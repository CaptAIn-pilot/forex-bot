# Forex Trading Glossary

This glossary explains key terms used in forex trading and automated trading systems.

## Trading Terms

**Ask Price**: The price at which a currency can be bought  
**Bid Price**: The price at which a currency can be sold  
**Spread**: The difference between the bid and ask price  
**Pip**: The smallest price move in a currency pair (usually 0.0001)  
**Lot**: A standard trading unit (typically 100,000 units of base currency)  
**Leverage**: Using borrowed capital to increase potential returns  
**Margin**: The deposit required to open a leveraged position  

## Currency Pairs

**Major Pairs**: Most traded pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, etc.)  
**Minor Pairs**: Cross-currency pairs without USD  
**Exotic Pairs**: Pairs including emerging market currencies  
**Base Currency**: The first currency in a pair (EUR in EUR/USD)  
**Quote Currency**: The second currency in a pair (USD in EUR/USD)  

## Risk Management

**Stop Loss**: An order to close a position when losses reach a certain level  
**Take Profit**: An order to close a position when profits reach a target  
**Risk/Reward Ratio**: Comparison of potential loss vs potential gain  
**Drawdown**: The decline from peak to trough in account value  
**Position Size**: The amount of capital allocated to a single trade  

## Technical Analysis

**Support**: A price level where buying interest is expected  
**Resistance**: A price level where selling interest is expected  
**Trend**: The general direction of price movement  
**Volatility**: The degree of price variation over time  
**Moving Average**: A trend-following indicator based on past prices  

## Automated Trading

**Algorithm**: A set of rules for making trading decisions  
**Backtesting**: Testing a strategy on historical data  
**Forward Testing**: Testing a strategy on new, unseen data  
**Slippage**: The difference between expected and actual execution price  
**Latency**: The delay between signal generation and trade execution  

## API and Technology

**API (Application Programming Interface)**: A way for programs to communicate  
**Real-time Data**: Live market prices and information  
**Historical Data**: Past market prices used for analysis  
**Webhook**: Automated messages sent when certain events occur  
**JSON**: A data format commonly used in APIs  

## Account Types

**Demo Account**: Practice account with virtual money  
**Live Account**: Real trading account with actual money  
**ECN Account**: Direct market access with tighter spreads  
**Standard Account**: Traditional broker account with fixed spreads  

## Order Types

**Market Order**: Immediate execution at current market price  
**Limit Order**: Execution only at a specified price or better  
**Stop Order**: Becomes a market order when price hits trigger level  
**Trailing Stop**: Stop loss that moves with favorable price movement
